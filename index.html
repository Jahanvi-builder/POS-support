How it works

  • Category tabs and sub-categories on one screen.
  • From the issue onwards, each answer stays on screen and the next step appears below it. Your selection stays pinned at the top.
  • Fix before request: the request only appears once fixes run out, or straight away for safety and damage.
  • Your steps: "Already tried any?" first, then one list that unlocks in order. The device checks itself where it can.
  • Restart: you come back to the same screen after the device boots.
